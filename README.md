# Figurines

A Vintage Story mod: carve figurines of the game's creatures in wood, cast them in metal, and collect the rare silver, gold and platinum poses. Creatures and metals are JSON, so other mods can add their own with one file.

- Mod page: https://mods.vintagestory.at/figurines
- Guide for modders: [MODDERS.md](MODDERS.md)
